# Salvation Journey

Salvation Journey is a third-person 3D Christian action-adventure game
currently in pre-production and early Unity development.

The player begins in Sinful Earth after receiving the message of salvation
and undertakes a dangerous journey toward the Celestial City.

Gameplay combines:

- third-person exploration
- physical and spiritual combat
- Health, Stamina and Resolve systems
- Prayer and Scripture-based abilities
- moral choices and persistent consequences
- a Testimony system that remembers the player's journey
- boss encounters based on spiritual themes

## Current Development

The project foundation is being built in Unity 6 using the Universal Render
Pipeline with Android as the primary target platform.

Current prototype work includes:

- project architecture
- player resource systems
- Resolve and Fear mechanics
- Prayer ability framework
- third-person movement
- combat prototype
- Gatekeeper of Fear vertical-slice encounter

## Current Prototype Goal

The first vertical slice follows Elias from the outskirts of Sinful Earth
through his first spiritual trial and culminates in a battle against the
Gatekeeper of Fear.

The prototype is designed to prove one of the game's core ideas:

> Different threats require different forms of overcoming.

Physical enemies may attack Health, while Fear, Deception and Despair can
attack Resolve and require Prayer, Scripture, Discernment or other spiritual
disciplines.
